How we work

Therapy grounded in evidence and relationship

Our approach draws from several established modalities, always adapted to what you need most. No two people are the same — neither are two therapy journeys.

Cognitive Behavioral
Identifying thought patterns that shape how you feel and behave — then building practical tools to shift them.
Person-Centered
You're the expert on your life. Our role is to create the conditions for growth, not to direct the path.
Mindfulness-Based
Developing present-moment awareness and self-compassion as a foundation for lasting change.
Trauma-Informed
All work is guided by an understanding of how past experiences live in the body and shape the present.
Who we work with

You might be a good fit if you're dealing with…

Anxiety & Stress
Persistent worry, panic, overwhelm, or burnout affecting your daily life and relationships.
Depression & Mood
Low motivation, sadness, or a sense of disconnection from yourself or the people around you.
Life Transitions
Career shifts, relationship changes, loss, or any moment where identity feels uncertain or unmoored.
Relationship Patterns
Communication struggles, boundary-setting, or repeating dynamics you'd like to understand and change.
