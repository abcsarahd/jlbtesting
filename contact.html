Common questions

Before you reach out

How do I know if therapy is right for me?

Therapy can be helpful for a wide range of experiences — from managing day-to-day stress to navigating major life transitions, anxiety, depression, grief, and relationship challenges. You don't need to be in crisis to benefit. If you're curious about how therapy might help, a free consultation is a great low-pressure place to start.

What happens in the first session?

The first session is a chance for us to get to know each other. We'll talk about what brought you in, your history, and what you'd like to work toward. There's no pressure to share everything at once — we go at your pace. By the end, we'll begin to outline some goals and discuss what ongoing sessions might look like.
