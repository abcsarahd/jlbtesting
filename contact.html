Before you reach out

How do I know if therapy is right for me?
If you're carrying something that feels heavy — and you're curious about having support — therapy is worth exploring. You don't need to be in crisis to start.
What happens in the first session?
The first session is mostly about getting to know each other. You'll share what brought you here, what you're hoping for, and we'll talk about how we might work together. There's no pressure to share everything right away.
