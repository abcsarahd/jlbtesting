Hey! Welcome to my page. I could spend the next few paragraphs telling you how wonderful I am, but I promise I'll try to keep the self-promotion to a minimum.

If you're here, there's probably a part of you that wants something to change. Maybe you're feeling stuck, overwhelmed, anxious, frustrated, or simply tired of dealing with the same problems over and over again. You may even know exactly what you should be doing, but knowing what to do and actually doing it are, unfortunately, two very different skill sets. Trust me, I think we can all relate to that.

My goal is to help you experience genuine optimism for your future, ideally starting from our very first meeting. Therapy should be a place where you can be honest about what's going on without feeling judged or like you need to have everything figured out. You don't need the perfect words, either. "I don't know" is a perfectly acceptable place to start.

I work best with people who genuinely want to get better but feel stuck using the same strategies that aren't working anymore. Sometimes our coping skills start creating more problems than they solve — we avoid, overthink, criticize ourselves, or try to control things that simply can't be controlled. The brain is a funny thing; it can be incredibly intelligent and still convince you that worrying about something for three hours is somehow productive. Together, we'll figure out what's keeping you stuck and develop strategies that actually help.

My approach is rooted in Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), which means therapy is less about me simply nodding along while you tell me what's going on and more about actually doing something with it. We'll look at the patterns that keep showing up, figure out what's driving them, and practice new ways of responding. Sometimes that means challenging the way you think, sometimes it means changing what you do, and sometimes it means learning to stop fighting battles that were never yours to win in the first place.

Most importantly, I want therapy to help you feel more capable of handling your life — not more dependent on therapy. The goal is to help you regain the confidence, power, and sense of control that you deserve.

You don't have to have your entire life figured out before you start. You just have to be willing to take the first step. We'll find the next ones together.

Mi objetivo es ayudarte a recuperar un sentido genuino de optimismo respecto a tu futuro, idealmente desde nuestra primera sesión. La terapia debe ser un espacio donde puedas ser honesto/a sobre lo que está pasando sin sentirte juzgado/a ni pensar que tienes que tener todo resuelto. Tampoco necesitas tener las palabras perfectas. "No sé" es un lugar perfectamente válido para comenzar.

Trabajo especialmente bien con personas que realmente quieren mejorar, pero sienten que están atrapadas utilizando las mismas estrategias que ya no están funcionando. A veces, nuestras formas de afrontar las cosas terminan creando más problemas de los que resuelven: evitamos, pensamos demasiado, nos criticamos o intentamos controlar cosas que simplemente no podemos controlar. El cerebro es algo curioso; puede ser increíblemente inteligente y aun así convencernos de que preocuparnos durante tres horas por algo es, de alguna manera, productivo. Juntos vamos a identificar qué te está manteniendo estancado/a y desarrollar estrategias que realmente te ayuden.

Mi enfoque está basado en los principios de la Terapia de Aceptación y Compromiso (ACT). Esto significa que la terapia no se trata simplemente de que yo asienta mientras me cuentas lo que está pasando, sino de que hagamos algo con ello. Analizaremos los patrones que siguen apareciendo, entenderemos qué los está impulsando y practicaremos nuevas formas de responder. A veces eso significa cuestionar la manera en que piensas, otras veces significa cambiar lo que haces, y otras veces significa aprender a dejar de luchar batallas que, para empezar, nunca te correspondieron.

Sobre todo, quiero que la terapia te ayude a sentirte más capaz de manejar tu propia vida, no más dependiente de la terapia. El objetivo es ayudarte a recuperar la confianza, el poder y la sensación de control que mereces.

No tienes que tener toda tu vida resuelta antes de comenzar. Solo tienes que estar dispuesto/a a dar el primer paso. Juntos encontraremos los siguientes.

Clinical orientation
My primary framework is Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) — an action-oriented approach focused on understanding the patterns that keep showing up, what's driving them, and practicing new ways of responding. Rather than trying to eliminate difficult thoughts and feelings, ACT helps you change your relationship with them so they have less power over your choices. I also draw on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Exposure & Response Prevention (ERP) for OCD, Trauma-Focused approaches, Solution-Focused Brief Therapy (SFBT), Psychodynamic and Person-Centered frameworks, and Multicultural and Reality Therapy perspectives — tailoring each session to what works best for you.

What does "Iterum" mean?

There's a reason I connect with the word Iterum, which means "again" or "anew." To me, it represents the idea that you don't have to stay stuck in the same patterns simply because they've been part of your life for a long time. You can try again. You can approach things differently. You can learn, grow, and create something new without having to become an entirely different person in the process. That's a big part of how I view therapy: not as starting your life over, but learning how to engage with it differently.
