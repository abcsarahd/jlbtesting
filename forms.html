Questions about paperwork?

If you are unsure which forms apply to your situation, or if you have any questions about what a form asks for, please do not hesitate to reach out to the office. A staff member will be happy to walk you through the process and make sure you have everything you need. We recommend completing your intake forms at least 24 hours before your first session so we can review them in advance — this helps us spend your appointment time focused on you rather than on administrative tasks.
